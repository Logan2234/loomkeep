Loomkeep stores no catalogue of its own: titles, covers and details come
live from open or public catalogues, which stay under their own terms. The
app lists the catalogues it queries in **Settings › Data sources**; JustWatch
is credited next to the offers it supplies.

## Catalogues

| Source                                                                                | Provides                                                                 | Terms                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <BrandIcon brand="tmdb" />[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org)                          | Films and series, cast, and where to watch                               | [TMDB's API terms](https://www.themoviedb.org/api-terms-of-use) |
| <BrandIcon brand="anilist" />[AniList](https://anilist.co)                            | Anime                                                                    | AniList's terms of use                                          |
| <BrandIcon brand="igdb" />[IGDB](https://www.igdb.com)                                | Games, and their time to beat                                            | IGDB's terms of use                                             |
| <BrandIcon brand="openlibrary" />[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org)              | Books and covers, from the Internet Archive                              | Catalogue data under CC0                                        |
| <BrandIcon brand="musicbrainz" />[MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org)               | Music                                                                    | Core data under CC0, the rest under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0             |
| <BrandIcon brand="coverartarchive" />[Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org) | Album covers, with MusicBrainz                                           | Each image under its own rights                                 |
| <BrandIcon brand="omdb" />[OMDb](https://www.omdbapi.com)                             | IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores, when the instance has a key | CC BY-NC 4.0                                                    |
| <BrandIcon brand="justwatch" />[JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com), through TMDB   | Streaming, rental and purchase offers                                    | JustWatch's attribution terms                                   |

**This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified,
or otherwise approved by TMDB.** That notice, required by TMDB's terms,
stays in English in every language of the app.

## Imports

Imports read your data from the other app, at your request: Steam through
its Web API, Simkl through its API with your permission, and every other
source from the export file you give. See [Importing](/guide/imports/).
