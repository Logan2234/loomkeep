Loomkeep reaches you three ways:

- **The bell**, in the app. Opening an entry, or clearing them all, removes
  it.
- **Push**, on your phone or computer, even with the app closed. It is
  turned on per device, in **Settings › Communications**.
- **Email**, for what must reach you wherever you are, starting with
  anything about your account's security.

What goes where is chosen in **Settings › Communications**. The tables below
list every alert, generated from the same list the app sends from.

<Aside title="Push on a phone">
  On iPhone and iPad, push only works once Loomkeep is [installed on the home
  screen](/guide/install/). On every device, push has to be turned on in each
  browser or installed app you use.
</Aside>

## Activity

What someone else did that concerns you. Everything lands in the bell, and
you choose what also comes as a push. On a new account those pushes start off;
accounts created before October 2026 had them all turned on.

<AlertTable group="activity" />

Reactions and upvotes stay in the bell: a count going up isn't worth a
notification on your phone.

## New episodes

New episodes aren't in the bell: the calendar and **Up next** already show
them. Instead, a summary of what came out goes by email, by push, or both,
weekly (Monday morning) or daily (in the evening, a premium feature to come).

## Security

Security alerts always go by email, which someone using your session can't
dismiss the way a bell entry is cleared. If you receive one for something you
didn't do, change your password and check **Settings › Security**.

## Administrators

Only an instance's administrators receive these. Email starts on and push
off; both can be switched in **Settings › Communications**.

<AlertTable group="admin" />

## On a self-hosted instance

Email and push depend on how the instance is set up: without an SMTP server,
no email goes out, and without push keys, no push. The bell always works. See
[Email and push](/self-hosting/email-and-push/).
