**What comes in:** your books with their status, rating, review, dates and
how many times you read them.

<Steps>

1. On The StoryGraph, open [Manage Account › Export StoryGraph Library](https://app.thestorygraph.com/user-export).
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › StoryGraph** and drop the `.csv`.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

Books are matched to Open Library, by ISBN when the export has one, by title
and author otherwise.
