**What comes in:** your watch history (films and episodes, with their dates
and rewatches), your watchlist with the date you added each title, your
favourites, and your ratings of films, series, seasons and episodes.

**Not imported yet:** your comments and reviews, custom lists, collection and
notes.

<Steps>

1. On Trakt, open [Settings › Data](https://trakt.tv/settings/data) and
   export your data. The export is free, unlike Trakt's API.
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Trakt** and drop the `.zip`.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

Trakt's export carries each title's TMDB, TVDB and IMDb ids, so most titles
match exactly.

A few things to know:

- **Rated, never watched:** Trakt lets you rate a title without marking it
  watched. Such a film or series joins your watchlist with its rating.
- **Episodes:** a rated episode you never marked as watched keeps its rating
  without counting as watched, so your progress stays as it was.
