**What comes in:** your books with their status, rating and review.

1. On [Babelio](https://www.babelio.com), open **Mes livres › Fonctionnalités
   avancées › Exporter ma bibliothèque**.
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Babelio** and drop the `.csv`.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

Babelio's export has no reminders, tags, loans or ownership. It can also list
a book you abandoned as read: check the statuses before importing.
