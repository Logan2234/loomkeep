**What comes in:** your books with their shelf (read, reading, to read),
rating, the date you finished them and how many times you read them. Your
private notes and your review become the book's private notes.

1. On Goodreads, open **My Books › Import and export** and choose **Export
   Library** ([Goodreads' help](https://help.goodreads.com/s/article/How-do-I-import-or-export-my-books-1553870934590)).
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Goodreads** and drop the `.csv`.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

Books are matched to Open Library, by ISBN when the export has one, by title
and author otherwise. Goodreads doesn't export when you started a book.
