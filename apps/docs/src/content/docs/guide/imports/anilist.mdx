**What comes in:** your anime list, with progress, scores, start and finish
dates, and notes. Custom lists and manga aren't imported.

<Steps>

1. Make sure your AniList profile isn't private, in your AniList settings,
   for the time of the import.
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › AniList** and enter your user name
   (or paste your profile's address, `anilist.co/user/…`).
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

There is nothing to match: AniList is the catalogue Loomkeep uses for anime,
so every title comes in as itself. Paused and rewatched titles come in as
watching, and a score is rounded to the nearest half point.
