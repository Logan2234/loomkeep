Simkl has no public profile or export file: Loomkeep reads your history by
connecting to your Simkl account, with your permission.

**What comes in:** films, series and anime, with your watchlist.

<Steps>

1. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Simkl** and choose **Sign in with
   Simkl**.
2. Allow Loomkeep on Simkl. You come back to Loomkeep, which fetches your
   history.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

<Aside title="Self-hosted instance">
  This source only shows up once the instance has its own Simkl app: see
  [Catalogue keys](/self-hosting/catalogues/#simkl).
</Aside>
