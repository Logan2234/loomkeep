Simkl has no public profile or export file: Loomkeep reads your history by
connecting to your Simkl account, with your permission.

**What comes in:** films, series and anime with every episode watched and
when, your watchlist with the date you added each title, your ratings, your
memos (as the entry's private note), and the series you dropped or put on
hold.

**Not imported:** rewatches, which Simkl only keeps on PRO accounts.

<Steps>

1. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Simkl** and choose **Sign in with
   Simkl**.
2. Allow Loomkeep on Simkl. You come back to Loomkeep, which fetches your
   history.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

A few things to know:

- **Statuses:** a series on hold comes in as being watched, and a dropped
  series or film as dropped. Other statuses follow from the episodes you
  watched.
- **Anime** are matched on TMDB, like the rest of Simkl's list. To keep
  AniList's data for them, import them from [AniList](/guide/imports/anilist/)
  or [MyAnimeList](/guide/imports/myanimelist/) too.

<Aside title="Self-hosted instance">
  This source only shows up once the instance has its own Simkl app: see
  [Catalogue keys](/self-hosting/catalogues/#simkl).
</Aside>
