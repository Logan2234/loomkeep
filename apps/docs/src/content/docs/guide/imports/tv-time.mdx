TV Time shut down on 15 July 2026. Its data came through the **GDPR request**
it had to answer: if you downloaded your `gdpr-data.zip` archive before the
closure, you can still import it.

**What comes in:** your series with every episode watched and when, your films
with their rewatches, your watchlist and the shows you set aside for later,
your favourites, your custom lists, your ratings of films and episodes, and
the date each title joined your TV Time library.

**What stays behind:** your reactions (the emotions you pick after an
episode), favourite characters, where you watched, friends and badges have no
equivalent in Loomkeep. The archive keeps no text for your comments. A show
you stopped following without ever starting it isn't imported.

<Steps>

1. Find the `gdpr-data.zip` archive TV Time emailed you.
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › TV Time** and drop the archive as
   it is, without unzipping it.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

Loomkeep matches TV Time's shows through their TVDB ids, so series land on
the right title even when names differ.

A few things to know:

- **Dates:** TV Time recorded when you ticked an episode or a film, not when
  you watched it. A season ticked in one go shares a single date.
- **Ratings:** TV Time's five steps, from "bad" to "wow", become 2, 4, 6, 8
  and 10 out of 10. An episode TV Time kept two different votes for stays
  unrated, since nothing tells which one is current.
- **Lists:** each custom list becomes a Loomkeep list with the same name. Its
  titles don't join your library. A list you already have under that name is
  left alone.
