**What comes in:** your rated films, series and episodes, your watchlist, and
your lists, with the notes you wrote on their titles. Video games, podcasts and
anything else IMDb tracks outside films and series aren't imported.

<Steps>

1. On IMDb, export your ratings, your watchlist and any list you want, from
   each page's **Export** option (or IMDb's **Exports** page). Each export is a
   CSV file.
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › IMDb** and drop all the CSV files at
   once.
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

</Steps>

IMDb's ids match exactly: there is nothing to confirm by hand. A title TMDB
doesn't know stays unmatched instead of being guessed from its name, and can
be searched by hand in the review step.

A few things to know:

- **Ratings** come in as they are: IMDb rates out of 10, like Loomkeep. A rated
  title counts as watched. IMDb only dates the rating, never the viewing, so
  the watch stays undated.
- **Series:** a rated series comes in as being watched, since rating a series
  says nothing about how far you got.
- **Episodes:** a rated episode is marked as watched on its series, and its
  rating goes to the episode. An episode TMDB can't place is skipped and
  counted among the unmatched titles.
- **Watchlist or list:** IMDb exports both with the same columns, so the file
  name tells them apart. Keep the watchlist file's name (it contains
  "watchlist"). Any other list file becomes a Loomkeep list named after the
  file, whose titles don't join your library.
- **Anime** are matched on TMDB, like everything IMDb exports. To keep AniList's
  data for them, import them from [AniList](/guide/imports/anilist/) or
  [MyAnimeList](/guide/imports/myanimelist/) too.
