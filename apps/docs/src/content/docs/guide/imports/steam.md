**What comes in:** the games you own, with the playtime Steam reports. You
choose each game's status while reviewing.

1. On Steam, make your **profile** and **game details** public, at least
   during the import, in your
   [privacy settings](https://steamcommunity.com/my/edit/settings).
2. In Loomkeep, open **Settings › Import › Steam** and enter your profile:
   its SteamID64 (`76561198…`) or its custom URL
   (`steamcommunity.com/id/username`).
3. Review and import: see [how an import works](/guide/imports/#how-an-import-works).

## Steam time and Loomkeep time

Steam's playtime and the [sessions](/guide/#sessions) you log in Loomkeep are
kept apart and never added up: a session may already be part of Steam's
time. **Update from Steam** refreshes Steam's playtime later; it never removes
a game or touches your statuses, notes, dates or sessions.
