## Domains

Loomkeep tracks four domains, each with its own library:

| Domain               | Catalogue                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Films, series, anime | TMDB, and AniList for anime |
| Games                | IGDB                        |
| Books                | Open Library                |
| Music (albums)       | MusicBrainz                 |

Turn on only the ones you use in **Settings › Domains**; the others disappear
from menus, search and stats. Catalogues are searched live: a title is only
copied into Loomkeep once someone tracks it.

## Statuses

Every title in your library has a status. Each domain has its own:

| Domain               | Not started | Under way | Finished              | Given up |
| -------------------- | ----------- | --------- | --------------------- | -------- |
| Films, series, anime | To watch    | Watching  | Completed, Up to date | Dropped  |
| Games                | Backlog     | Playing   | Completed             | Dropped  |
| Books                | To read     | Reading   | Read                  | Dropped  |
| Music                | To listen   | —         | Listened              | —        |

For films, series and anime, the status **follows from what you watch**:
tick episodes and a series moves to Watching, then Completed. **Up to date**
means you have seen every episode of a show that is still airing. Only
**Dropped** is set by hand.

## Rewatches and cycles

Watching a film again, replaying a game or rereading a book starts a new
**cycle**: the first viewing, playthrough or reading stays in your history,
and the new one is counted separately. For series, each episode keeps every
time you watched it.

## Sessions

For games and books, you can log **sessions**: a date, a length, pages read
for a book, and notes. Sessions belong to the playthrough or reading under
way, add up to your time spent and feed your stats. The timer
logs one for you while you play or read.

Logging sessions is optional: a book marked Read without any session still
counts as finished.

## History

Everything you watch, play, read and listen to is dated, so your history and
stats show when, not just what. Imported titles sometimes come without dates:
they still count, but can't show up on a given day.
