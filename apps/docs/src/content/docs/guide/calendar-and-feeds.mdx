Loomkeep gives you private links that other apps subscribe to. They refresh
on their own: new episodes show up without importing anything again.

Tracked movies also appear in the calendar at their local cinema release, or
digital release when no cinema date is announced. They stay listed whether or
not a release reminder is enabled. A movie without a confirmed local date waits
until one is announced.

Tracked games appear on their release day, or across their month when only the
month is announced. A game dated to a quarter or a year waits for a closer date.

| Link              | What it holds                                     | Where to find it                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Calendar**      | Upcoming episodes, movie and game releases         | **Add to my calendar** on the calendar page, or **Settings › Integrations**            |
| **Release feed**  | Episodes out over the last 30 days, RSS or Atom   | The same window, under the calendar link                                               |
| **Activity feed** | Your activity: additions, progress, reviews, lists | **Activity feed** on your profile, or **Settings › Integrations**, when social is on   |

## Adding your calendar

Copy the calendar link from Loomkeep, then subscribe to it in your app.
Subscribe rather than import: an imported file never updates.

<Tabs syncKey="calendar-app">
  <TabItem label="Google Calendar">
    <Steps>

    1. On a computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
    2. Next to **Other calendars**, choose **+**, then **From URL**.
    3. Paste the link and choose **Add calendar**.

    </Steps>

    It then appears on your phone too. Google refreshes subscriptions every
    few hours, so a new episode can take a while to show up.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Apple Calendar">
    <Steps>

    1. On a Mac, choose **File › New Calendar Subscription**. On an iPhone or
       iPad, open **Settings › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Add Account ›
       Other › Add Subscribed Calendar** (under **Settings › Apps** in recent
       versions).
    2. Paste the link.
    3. On a Mac, set **Auto-refresh** to every hour or every day.

    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Outlook">
    <Steps>

    1. In [Outlook on the web](https://outlook.live.com/calendar/), choose
       **Add calendar**, then **Subscribe from web**.
    2. Paste the link, give it a name, and choose **Import**.

    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Thunderbird">
    <Steps>

    1. In the calendar, choose **New Calendar**, then **On the Network**.
    2. Paste the link as the location, and follow the steps.

    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Following a feed

Paste the release or activity link into a feed reader: Feedly, FreshRSS,
Inoreader, NetNewsWire, Thunderbird… Pick **RSS** or **Atom** for the
release feed, whichever your reader prefers; the activity feed is Atom.

## Keeping them private

Anyone with one of these links can read what it holds, without signing in.
Don't share them. If one leaks, **regenerate** it from the same place: the
old link stops working at once, and every app subscribed to it needs the new
one. The calendar and release feed share one link: regenerating one
regenerates both.

For scripts and dashboards that need more than these views, see the
[API](/api/).
