Loomkeep has an Android app. It shows the same Loomkeep as the website, so
new features reach it as soon as they reach the site, and adds what a web
app can't do:

- **Notifications** from Android itself, sorted into channels (episodes,
  messages…) you can tune in Android's settings.
- **Scanner**: a book's barcode or a friend's profile QR code, straight
  from the camera.
- **Share to Loomkeep**: share a link from IMDb, Letterboxd or your browser,
  and Loomkeep opens the work.
- **Links and shortcuts**: links from Loomkeep's emails open in the app, and
  a long press on its icon jumps to search, the calendar or your stats.
- **Home-screen widgets**: what to watch next, and what comes out today.

It isn't on Google Play yet: you install it from GitHub.

## Install

<Steps>

1. On your phone, download
   [**loomkeep-android.apk**](https://github.com/Logan2234/loomkeep/releases/latest/download/loomkeep-android.apk),
   the latest release.
2. Open it. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your
   browser or file manager: allow it, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, open Loomkeep and sign in.

</Steps>

The app needs Android 7.0 or later and an up-to-date **Android System
WebView** (from Google Play); with an older one, it says so and links to the
update.

<Aside>
  Google Play Protect may warn that the app comes from an unknown developer:
  it only means the app wasn't installed from Google Play. Every release is
  built from this repository by GitHub Actions.
</Aside>

## Keep it up to date

Most changes need nothing from you: the app loads Loomkeep from the server,
like the website. A new version of the app itself only comes out when its
Android side changes.

Android doesn't update apps installed outside Google Play on its own.
[Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev) does it for you: add
`https://github.com/Logan2234/loomkeep` as an app, and it installs each new
release over the previous one, keeping you signed in. You can also download
the new APK from the link above and install it over the old one.

## Widgets

Long-press an empty spot of your home screen, pick **Widgets**, then
Loomkeep:

- **To watch**: your shows in progress and their next episode. The ✓ marks
  it watched without opening the app; tap a show to open it.
- **Releases**: what comes out today among what you follow, else what comes
  out next.

Each one grows with its size, from a single show or release up to six.
They show posters by default: long-press a widget, then its settings, to
switch it to a list. They refresh every half hour, and as soon as you leave
the app. Signing out of the app signs them out too.

## Your own instance

The app opens loomkeep.app by default. To use a
[self-hosted](/self-hosting/) instance, tap **Use another instance** under
the sign-in form, and enter its address (it must use https). The same
screen takes you back to loomkeep.app, and the page shown when Loomkeep
can't load offers it too.

On another instance, two things don't work yet:

- **Push notifications**: the app's notifications go through loomkeep.app's
  keys, which another instance doesn't have. Alerts still come by email,
  and the installed [web app](/guide/install/) can get push notifications
  if the instance has them set up.
- **Links from emails** open in the browser rather than in the app.
