## Is Loomkeep free?

Yes. Everything works for free, without ads. A premium plan is planned: it
will sell comfort and higher limits, never take away what you already have
for free. Until it exists, every feature is open to everyone.

## Are there ads? How is Loomkeep funded?

No ads, and no advertising trackers. Loomkeep is made and hosted by one
person; [donations](/project/#supporting-the-project) pay for the server,
and the premium plan will, once it exists.

## Why can't I find a title?

- **Its domain is off**: turn it on in **Settings › Domains**.
- **It's an adult title**: those are hidden unless you allow them in
  **Settings › Content**, which needs your date of birth.
- **It isn't in the catalogue**: Loomkeep searches TMDB, AniList, IGDB, Open
  Library and MusicBrainz live, and shows what they have. A title missing
  there is missing here; you can add it to that catalogue yourself.

## Why does my series say "Up to date"? Why can't I pick its status?

For films, series and anime, the status follows from what you have watched:
tick episodes and it moves on its own. **Up to date** means you have seen
every episode of a show that is still airing; it becomes **Completed** when
the show ends. Only **Dropped** is set by hand. See
[Key concepts](/guide/#statuses).

## An import matched the wrong title. How do I fix it?

The review step before importing is where to fix matches: search and pick
the right title there. Once imported, remove the wrong title from your
library and add the right one.

## Who can see my library?

In **Settings › Privacy**, you choose who can find and follow you: everyone,
only the friends you approve, or nobody (ghost mode). You also choose, for
each domain, who sees your library and your activity. Your notes are always private. On an
instance with social features off, nobody else sees anything.

## Can anyone read my messages?

Only the friend you're writing to, and the moderators of a message one of you
reports. Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, though: whoever hosts the
instance could technically read them. See [Messages](/guide/messages/).

## Can I move from loomkeep.app to my own instance?

Not in one step yet. Meanwhile, the **complete archive** in
**Settings › Export** keeps everything you have, and books move across
through the Goodreads export and import. See [Your data](/guide/your-data/).

## Does Loomkeep work offline?

The app opens without a connection once [installed](/guide/install/), but
needs one to load and save your library.
